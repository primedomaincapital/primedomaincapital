About Us

We believe the finest .com domains are irreplaceable digital property — and we hold them as such.

Prime Domain Capital was founded on a simple observation: in every industry, one address commands disproportionate trust. We acquire those addresses, hold them as private property, and make select holdings available for direct purchase.

Holding Philosophy

Four principles govern every acquisition.

We are deliberately selective. Most domains we review do not meet the standard below — and that selectivity is the strategy.

I

Category definition, not keywords

We buy names that are the category in the customer's mind — not clever variations of it. When someone thinks "private debt data" or "AI audit trail," the .com should end the search. Exact-match authority compounds; cleverness depreciates.

II

Structural demand, not cycles

We select for demand measured in decades: the growth of private credit, the digitization of finance, the regulation of artificial intelligence, the financing of the space economy. Fashions fade; infrastructure persists.

III

Scarcity is the moat

There is exactly one privatedebtdata.com. No competitor can build, buy, or borrow an equivalent — they can only bid for this one, or settle for less. That asymmetry is the entire reason we hold them, and it strengthens every year as the category matures.

IV

Patience as a position

We hold until the right steward arrives — the company for whom the name is transformative, not merely useful. A forced sale captures a fraction of the asset's worth. Conviction, carried quietly, captures the rest.

Our Approach

Acquire with rigor. Hold with conviction. Sell directly.

Everything we hold was acquired under the same discipline: buy only what is irreplaceable, hold with conviction, and sell directly at a fixed price.
