The firm

We buy names the way a collector buys art — for permanence.

Prime Domain Capital is a private company that owns thirty-eight of the finest category-defining .com domains on the internet. We sell only what we own. We do not broker for others. We do not advise, consult, or manage assets for clients. We hold — and when the right steward arrives, we transfer.

Our portfolio spans eight market categories: capital and credit, wealth technology, artificial intelligence, energy and resources, price intelligence, healthcare, legal and compliance, and consumer brands. The combined listed value is $621,588.

Every holding passed the same test before we acquired it: would the leading company in this industry be stronger owning this name than anyone else? If the answer was yes, we bought it. If the answer is ever no, we sell it — to the company for whom it is transformative.

I.

Hold, don't flip

We are not domainers chasing volume. Each name is underwritten like a permanent asset and held until the right owner — not the first bidder — arrives.

II.

Price once, price honestly

Every holding carries a single fixed Buy Now price. No auctions, no hidden reserves, no manufactured urgency. The number reflects the asset's worth.

III.

Transfer cleanly

Every sale closes through Escrow.com with verified funds and a clean registrar transfer. Confidentiality is standard, not requested.

What we are not

Clarity is a courtesy.

Prime Domain Capital is a private holder selling its own assets. We are not an investment adviser, a broker-dealer, a consultant, or a service provider of any kind. Nothing on this site is investment advice, an offer of securities, or a solicitation of any regulated activity. We sell domain names — nothing more, nothing less.

On value

Listed prices reflect our assessment of each name's strategic worth to its ideal acquirer — the company for whom the domain is transformative rather than merely useful. Comparable category-defining .com sales inform every price, but each name is unique and past sales do not predict future ones.

A forced sale captures a fraction of an asset's worth. Conviction, carried quietly, captures the rest. We hold until the right steward arrives.
