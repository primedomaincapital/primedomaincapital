Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Prime Domain Capital is a private holder of domain names. This website exists to present those holdings for sale. We collect as little information as possible, and we do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information We Collect

When you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send — typically your name, email address, and the content of your inquiry. We use that information solely to respond to your inquiry and, if you proceed, to complete a domain transaction.

This website also uses privacy-respecting web analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns (such as which pages are visited). Analytics data is aggregated and does not identify individual visitors.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries about our domain holdings
  • To facilitate domain sales, including through escrow services
  • To maintain and improve this website

Information Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties except as necessary to complete a transaction you have requested (for example, an escrow provider) or as required by law. Domain transactions conducted through escrow are governed by the escrow provider's own privacy terms.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you send us. Email correspondence is held in confidence and is never published or disclosed to other parties.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. Analytics, if enabled, uses first-party measurement only.

Your Rights

You may request that we delete correspondence you have sent us by writing to the contact address below. We will comply promptly except where retention is required for completed transaction records.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to info@primedomaincapital.com.
