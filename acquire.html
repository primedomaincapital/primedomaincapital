Questions

Asked plainly,
answered plainly.

Is the listed price negotiable? +

Each price is set against comparable sales of category-defining .com domains and reflects the name's strategic worth to its ideal acquirer. We price once and price honestly — the number you see is the number.

How does the transfer work? +

Through Escrow.com. You deposit the purchase price; Escrow.com verifies the funds; we transfer the domain to the registrar account you designate; Escrow.com releases the funds to us. You are never exposed, and neither are we.

Can I pay over time? +

Lease-to-own terms up to sixty months are available on qualifying holdings through our marketplace partners. Write to us and we will arrange it.

Will my inquiry remain confidential? +

Absolutely. We never disclose who inquired about what, and we never use one party's interest as leverage with another. Discretion is how we do business.

Do you broker domains for others? +

No. We sell only what we own — the forty-seven holdings in this portfolio. We are not brokers, advisors, or intermediaries of any kind.
