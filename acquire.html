How to Acquire

From inquiry to ownership,
in days, not months.

Every domain we hold is available for direct purchase at a fixed price. Three paths lead to the same secure, escrow-protected transfer.

01 — Browse

Start with what we hold.

Thirty-eight premium .com domains across eight categories — each listed at a fixed Buy Now price. When one sells, it leaves the collection permanently; there is no second copy.

Every listing shows the full price up front. No auctions, no negotiation theater — the number reflects what the name is worth.

  • Fixed Buy Now pricing on all 38 holdings
  • Eight categories: capital, wealth tech, AI, energy, pricing, healthcare, legal & compliance, consumer
  • Full description of each name's category position
02 — Purchase

Three paths to ownership.

Buy instantly through a marketplace, or contact us directly for a private sale. All three paths close through the same secure, escrow-protected process.

Marketplace purchases complete through Afternic or Sedo checkout. Direct purchases are arranged by email at info@primedomaincapital.com and closed through Escrow.com.

  • Afternic — instant Buy Now checkout
  • Sedo — Buy Now with secure transfer
  • Direct — email us to arrange a private sale
03 — Transfer

Secured through escrow.

Every acquisition closes through Escrow.com, the industry standard for domain transfers. Funds are verified before the transfer begins — neither party carries counterparty risk.

Once payment is secured, we initiate the registrar transfer and provide the authorization code. Most transfers complete within days.

  • Funds verified before transfer begins
  • Authorization code provided promptly
  • Transfer typically completes within days
