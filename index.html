The Thesis

Location, location, location.

Every industry has an address. The firms that own the definitive one rarely have to introduce themselves.

A category-defining .com does what prime real estate has always done: it places its owner at the center of the market's attention, permanently. Customers arrive already convinced. Partners assume scale. Competitors price around you.

We evaluate domains the way one evaluates prime property — on the durability of the location, not the fashion of the moment. Exact-match names in markets with structural, decades-long demand: private credit, wealth technology, artificial intelligence, critical resources, and the emerging discipline of price transparency.

Twenty-nine holdings. Seven categories. One standard: would the leading company in this industry be stronger owning this name than anyone else? If the answer is yes, we acquire it — and we hold until the right owner arrives.
