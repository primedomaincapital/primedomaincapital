The thesis

A great domain is not a web address.
It is a position.

Every industry has a small number of names that describe the entire category — the phrase a buyer types before they know which company they want. Whoever owns that phrase owns the front door.

Prime Domain Capital exists for one reason: to hold those front doors. We acquire category-defining .com domains, we hold them with conviction, and we transfer them — cleanly, confidentially, at a fixed price — to the company for whom the name is transformative.

"We do not sell domains. We transfer positions of permanent strategic advantage."

We are not brokers. We are not advisors. We are not a marketplace. We are a private holder selling only what we own — and we have spent years assembling forty-seven of the finest category names on the internet.
